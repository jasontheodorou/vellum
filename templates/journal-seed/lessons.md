# Lessons

Grouped by topic. Each entry is one of four kinds:

- **Lesson:** a principle learned the hard way.
- **Insight:** a new way of seeing a problem.
- **Technique:** how to do something, step by step.
- **Pattern:** a structure worth reusing.

Each entry should make sense on its own, without the session it came from.
